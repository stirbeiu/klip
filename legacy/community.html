Community Guidelines

klip is a community where people come to share, learn, and be entertained. To keep this community safe and enjoyable for everyone, we ask that you follow these guidelines.

1. Respect Everyone

We have zero tolerance for harassment, bullying, or hate speech. Treat others with the respect you expect for yourself.

2. No Spam

Do not post repetitive, unwanted, or misleading content. This includes scams and misleading metadata.

3. Safe Content Only

Do not post content that is violent, sexually explicit, or illegal. We want klip to be safe for a wide audience.

Violation of these guidelines may result in account termination.
